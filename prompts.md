# SWOT Prompt:
### This prompt helps to analyze a problem statement's Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats.

"I have a Problem Statement from SIH (or hackathon) that my team is considering. Please run a detailed SWOT analysis on it, specific to a hackathon context, not a generic business SWOT.

Go deep on each quadrant with specific, non-generic points:

```
💪 Strengths
- What skills, prior projects, or domain knowledge would a team need for this, and where do we already have an edge?
- What makes this problem statement easier to execute well compared to others in the same category?

⚠️ Weaknesses
- What is the most likely skill or resource gap that could block us mid-build?
- Where are we weakest compared to a team that specializes in this exact domain?

💡 Opportunities
- Is this a problem statement most teams will underestimate or avoid, and why?
- Does this open the door to a genuinely new angle or technology judges have not seen much of yet?

🔥 Threats
- How many other teams are likely to pick this same problem statement, and how would we differentiate?
- What is the one thing that could make this scope balloon out of control during the hackathon?

Give me the output as a clean 2x2 grid, followed by a one-line verdict: is this SWOT favorable enough to proceed, or should we look at alternate problem statements?"
```
